PDF to Word (.docx)

Convert PDF pages to an editable Word document (.docx) entirely in this tab. Your PDF is never uploaded.

Drop PDF files here, or click to choose

How this works

This tool uses PDF.js to read the PDF file and extract text from each page. The extracted text is then assembled into a Word document using the docx library. Everything happens locally in your browser — no upload to a server.

Common uses

Converting scanned reports or PDF forms into an editable Word file, extracting content for further editing, or repurposing PDF content for newsletters and presentations.
