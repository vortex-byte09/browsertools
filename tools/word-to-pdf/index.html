Word to PDF (.pdf)

Convert Word (.docx) files to PDF entirely in this tab. Your document is never uploaded.

Drop Word files here, or click to choose

How this works

This tool uses Mammoth.js to convert the Word document to HTML, then uses jsPDF and html2canvas to convert that HTML to a PDF file. All processing happens locally in your browser — no upload to a server.

Common uses

Converting Word reports, resumes, or letters to PDF for sharing or printing, ensuring the document looks the same on any device.
